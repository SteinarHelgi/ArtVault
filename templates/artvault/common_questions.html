Common Questions

Buying artwork

How do I buy artwork?

ArtVault is an auction platform. To purchase artwork, you place a bid on the piece you are interested in. The highest bidder at the end of the auction wins the artwork. You can always place a higher bid if someone outbids you.

Can I place bids?

Yes. You need a buyer account to place bids on artwork. If you do not already have one, you can sign up as a buyer.

How do I track my bids?

Go to your My Bids page to view an overview of all your bids and their current status.

Can I cancel a bid?

Once a bid has been submitted, it is binding. However, before confirming a bid, you can choose not to continue.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit card payments, bank transfers, and wire transfers.

Is shipping included in the price?

Yes. We offer free shipping, so you only pay for the artwork.

Artists & Sellers

How do I upload artwork?

You need a seller or gallery account to upload artwork. If you do not already have one, you can sign up as a seller or gallery.

Can you edit listings after posting?

Listings can be edited before the auction begins. Once the auction has started, listings can only be deleted, not edited.

How long do auctions last?

Auction length varies. When creating a listing, the seller chooses the start and end date.

Account

Do I need an account to buy artwork?

Yes. You need a buyer account to place bids and purchase artwork.

How do I reset my password?

You can change your password in your account settings.
